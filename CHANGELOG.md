# Changelog

## Unreleased

First public release for WoW Forever.

- "AH Scan" button on the Baganator bank and bag windows.
  - Left-click: searches every distinct item on the auction house through a temporary Auctionator list.
  - Right-click: opens the options.
- Works for any character selected in Baganator, not only the logged-in one.
- Options: show the buttons only at the auction house, filter by rarity, scan only equipment /
  crafting reagents / consumables, list of excluded items (name, itemID or Shift+click).
- Bound items are always skipped.
- Translated into English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian,
  Korean and Chinese (simplified and traditional).
