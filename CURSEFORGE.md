**Baganator AH** adds an **AH Scan** button to the [Baganator](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/baganator) bank and bag windows.

* **Left-click**: every distinct item of the displayed bank or bags is searched on the auction house, through a temporary [Auctionator](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/auctionator) shopping list.
* **Right-click**: options.

Works with **any of your characters**: select an alt in Baganator, and its bank or bags are scanned from Baganator's cache (Syndicator). If the auction house is closed, the search starts the next time you open it.

### Options
* Show the buttons only when the auction house is open
* Filter by rarity
* Scan only equipment, crafting reagents and/or consumables
* Excluded items (name, itemID or Shift+click)

Bound items are always skipped, since they cannot be sold.

### Requirements
Baganator, Syndicator and Auctionator.

### Commands
`/bah options`, `/bah scan [bank|bags] [Name-Realm]`, `/bah quality <0-5>`, `/bah who`, `/bah debug`
